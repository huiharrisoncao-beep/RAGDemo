# Company and Subsidiary Structure

This document only describes the ownership and subsidiary structure of the Xinghuan Technology group. It contains no executive or personnel information.

## Xinghuan Technology

Xinghuan Technology is a diversified technology group operating across artificial intelligence, data platforms, and network communications. It manages several independently operated subsidiaries through a holding structure.

## Yunshu Intelligence

Yunshu Intelligence is a wholly owned subsidiary of Xinghuan Technology, focused on enterprise-grade AI inference platforms.

## Hengxing Data

Hengxing Data is a subsidiary of Xinghuan Technology, specializing in large-scale data warehouses and real-time analytics engines.

## Guangnian Network

Guangnian Network is also part of Xinghuan Technology, serving as the group's subsidiary for network communications and edge computing.
